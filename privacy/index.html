Privacy Policy

Receipted for iPhone · Effective November 13, 2026

Receipted is built to keep your receipts yours. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. This page explains exactly what leaves your phone, and why.

What stays on your phone

Your receipts, their photos and details, saved cards (a nickname and the last 4 digits only), clients, reports and settings are stored only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups, if you use them). Deleting the app deletes them from the phone.

Reading receipts with AI

When you scan a receipt, its page photos and your list of expense types are sent over an encrypted connection to Receipted's server (hosted by Supabase in the United States). The server passes them to Anthropic's AI model, which reads the merchant, date, total and tax and sends the result back to your phone. Receipted's server does not store your photos or what was read from them.

Anthropic processes this under its commercial terms: it does not use it to train its models, and may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

For a no-receipt photo (a gas pump or parking screen), only that photo is sent. Your location is never sent to Receipted's server.

Daily limits

To keep the service affordable, the server counts AI reads per day. It uses a random ID made by the app when it's installed (not linked to you, your device or your Apple ID) and your network address. Neither is stored as-is: each is turned into a one-way code before it's counted, so it can't be read back. Only these coded daily counts are kept, and they are deleted after 30 days.

Location

Receipted asks for your location only when you use No-Receipt Capture. It is used on your phone, with Apple's map services, to find the merchant and address. It is saved with that receipt on your phone and printed on its missing-receipt page, which goes only where you send it.

OneDrive (Receipted Plus)

If you turn on auto-upload, receipt files go directly from your phone to your own OneDrive. They never pass through Receipted's server. Sign-in is handled by Microsoft, and the resulting sign-in tokens are kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

  • Microsoft: permission to read and write files in your OneDrive (to save receipts) and to read your basic profile (to show which account is signed in).

Receipted never sees your password. Signing out deletes the tokens from your phone.

Sharing and exports

Files you share or save, such as receipts, reports and spreadsheets, go only where you choose.

Purchases

Receipted Plus is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment, and Receipted never receives your payment details.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

Receipted has no sign-up, shows no ads, uses no analytics or advertising tools, and does not track you across apps or websites. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Children

Receipted is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.
