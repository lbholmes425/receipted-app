Support

Help with Receipted for iPhone

Contact: barton@holmesdg.com. Usually answered within 2 business days. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Turning AI reading on or off

Receipted asks before the first receipt is read by AI. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Read receipts with AI. With it off, nothing is sent and you type the details in yourself.

The AI read something wrong

Everything on the Review screen can be edited before you tap Looks good. Fields the AI wasn't sure about are flagged. Long receipts read best when every page is scanned in order, with the totals page included.

There's no receipt (gas pump, parking, drive-thru)

Tap + → No receipt and photograph the screen showing the amount. Receipted fills in the merchant and address from your location, and the file includes a missing-receipt page with the time and place.

How are receipts named and filed?

Set each card's statement closing day in Settings → Cards. Receipts are filed into that statement's folder. Folder and file-name styles are in Settings → Folders & File Names.

OneDrive says “Approval required”

Some companies only allow approved apps to use their Microsoft accounts. Ask your IT team to approve Receipted for OneDrive file access. It needs only: User.Read, Files.ReadWrite and offline_access. Until then, use Share or Save to Files on any receipt.

Uploads are paused

Open Settings → Auto-upload → OneDrive. The paused box says why (signed out, folder removed, approval needed) and has the button that fixes it. Nothing is lost: receipts wait on your phone and upload once it's sorted.

I deleted a receipt that was already uploaded

Receipted never deletes files from your cloud folder. It renames them to start with VOID_ so accounting can see the receipt was withdrawn.

Free reads, Receipted Plus and subscriptions

The free version includes 15 AI receipt reads a month, and you can always type receipts in by hand. Receipted Plus adds AI reading for every receipt (with a generous daily fair-use limit), monthly reports (PDF and spreadsheet; free users can preview them), auto-upload to OneDrive, and your own folders, file names, expense types and labels. To restore a purchase, use Settings → Get Receipted Plus → Restore Purchases. To cancel, use Manage Subscription there, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Deleting your data

Your receipts live only on your phone. Delete a receipt by swiping it, or delete the app to remove everything. Files already uploaded to your OneDrive stay there until you delete them.
