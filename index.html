Receipted

Scan it, file it, done — receipts for your corporate card.

  • Scan any receipt, including long multi-page ones. The total, date, merchant and tax are read for you.
  • No receipt? Photograph the gas pump or parking screen. Receipted fills in the merchant and place from where you are and adds a missing-receipt page ready for you to sign.
  • Filed by statement. Each receipt is named and sorted into your card's statement period.
  • Sends itself. With Receipted Plus, receipts upload straight to your OneDrive folder.
  • Monthly report as a PDF and a spreadsheet, ready for accounting (Receipted Plus).
